5. DAILY DROP — SIGNATURE EXPERIENCE
======================================================================

- Exactly ONE global editorial music question per day, published at 00:00 UTC. It is the same question worldwide, not a different question by country/time zone.
- Members can read other answers without first answering. Answers are attributable to profiles (no anonymous answers).
- Answers are text-first, with optional artist/album/track references; allow replies and reactions.
- Keep a permanent, fully interactive archive, subject to lawful deletion and moderation.
- Provide personal Daily Drop answer history on member profiles with privacy controls.
- Support sorting: For You, Recent, Most Discussed. Discussion ranking should reflect meaningful genuine discussion, not simply reaction counts, and avoid burying new/quiet members.
- Members MAY suggest Daily Drop questions. Administrators review, edit, translate, approve and schedule; there is NO automatic member-vote selection or community-controlled publication.
- One optional recurring Daily Drop push notification is important: opt-in/permission required, delivered at each member's chosen local time AFTER that day's global question has published. Handle time zones and daylight saving time; notification delivery is best effort, not guaranteed. This is distinct from optional social-interaction notifications.
- Editorial continuity: automatic failure detection, safe retry, duplicate prevention, admin alerts and a reserve of approved Daily Drop questions.
