## Checked and found fine (reviewer)
Only the owner gets a copy (HMAC over id, owner and expiry, constant-time compare, session user, `exportArchive` filters owner, status and expiry, one 404 for every failure, `private, no-store`). The confirmation cannot be skipped (`verify-password` is server-only; the Google-only path needs a sign-in younger than 15 minutes). No CSRF (server actions with Next's origin check) and no open redirect (`reauthAction` returns to `/settings/data` only; `account-closed` takes a regex-checked date). Status gates hold for writes; `settings/security` stays usable in the grace period (password and 2FA changes, as the email advises). Restore cannot race the purge (`FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, the request and the status re-checked, a due request cannot be restored); two workers are safe. The export carries no reporter identity and only @usernames for relationships. ADM-15 is admin-only on pages and actions; the address shows only with "reply by email". The new pages are on the private (no-store) paths. Logs carry summaries only.
