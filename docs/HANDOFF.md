## Lessons (session 40)

## How things work (the parts a new session needs)

Ids are UUIDs generated by PostgreSQL.
- **Accounts come from invitations only (step 5.4):** `/invite/<token>` sets the `gr_invite` cookie and redirects to `/join`; the pre-account steps (18+, language, account) keep nothing on the server; `createAccountAction` calls `signUpEmail` (the hooks check the cookie, verify the account and accept the invitation) and then `signInEmail`; the post-account steps write through `src/lib/data/onboarding.ts` and move `profile.onboarding_step`; `memberPage` redirects to that step until `onboarded_at` is set.
